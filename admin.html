GOVERNANCE & PERMISSIONS

Designations & access

Management titles map to least-privilege tool permissions.
Designation assignment must be performed by an authorized system owner. A user cannot grant themselves a higher title or permissions by changing a profile field.

Suggested designation and permission matrix

DesignationTypical scopeRecommended access
CEOEnterprise strategy and governanceExecutive KPIs and approved reports; no default access to unrestricted recordings or employee credentials
COOCompany-wide operational performanceCross-operation KPIs, capacity, escalations, policy oversight
Operation President (OP)Business-unit operational leadershipAssigned business-unit performance, service levels, QA trends
Operation Vice-President (OVP)Multi-site or multi-program oversightAssigned programs, staffing, performance, escalation reports
Operation Manager (OM)Program or department deliveryTeam staffing, skills/tasks, performance, QA results, authorized call reviews
Assistant Operation Manager (Assistant OM)Delegated operations supportScoped staffing and performance actions delegated by OM
SupervisorTeam-level coaching and deliveryTeam assignments, attendance/status views, QA reviews, coaching follow-ups
Team LeaderDaily workflow and team supportTeam task coordination, skill visibility, limited team performance views
TrainerOnboarding and skill developmentTraining assignments, learning progress, approved coaching examples
Subject Matter Expert (SME)Process and product expertiseKnowledge resources, assigned escalations, scoped quality feedback

This is a proposed baseline. Final access must also be scoped by program, team, and data sensitivity. Executive title alone should not automatically grant unrestricted call audio access.
